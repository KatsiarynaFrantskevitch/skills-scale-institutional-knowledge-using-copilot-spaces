# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction, maintain delivery health, and support the team in making sound implementation decisions. They balance technical quality, delivery speed, and cross-functional alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide technical architecture, standards, and implementation patterns
- Help estimate complexity, identify dependencies, and reduce delivery risk
- Support engineering execution, mentoring, and technical decision-making
- Partner with Product Manager on trade-offs, feasibility, and sequencing
- Coordinate with Project Manager on milestones, risks, and cross-team dependencies
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure testability and quality outcomes

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and reliable technical solutions
- Improve team execution and reduce avoidable rework
- Create alignment between product goals and engineering realities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Dependency tracking with PM and cross-functional teams
- Design discussions with Product Manager and Developers

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers ensure the solution is usable, valuable, and grounded in user needs. They help translate customer pain points into clear experience decisions and measurable usability outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Capture user needs, behavior, and feedback through research and testing
- Design flows, interactions, and interface decisions that support the product goal
- Help refine acceptance criteria around usability and customer experience
- Partner with Product Manager to validate priorities and assumptions
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure feasibility and implementation fidelity
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to validate user experience quality

### Goals
- Improve adoption, satisfaction, and usability
- Reduce ambiguity in product decisions through evidence and user insight
- Ensure customer experience is considered alongside technical delivery

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and research sessions
- Product review meetings and UX feedback loops
- Collaboration with Developers on implementation details
- Quality validation and usability testing with QA

---

## Technical Writer / Documentation Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Writers ensure project outcomes are understandable, usable, and well-documented for internal teams and end users. They help capture decisions, operating guidance, and release communication.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user-facing and operational documentation
- Draft release notes, onboarding guides, and process documentation
- Review product changes for clarity, completeness, and consistency
- Coordinate with Product Manager on messaging and stakeholder readiness
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm what changed and what needs to be documented
- Support project continuity by capturing decisions and institutional knowledge

### Goals
- Improve clarity, adoption, and team alignment
- Reduce confusion for users, support teams, and future contributors
- Ensure documentation keeps pace with delivery

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness checkpoints
- Documentation reviews with Developers and PM
- Cross-functional review of project updates and launch content

---

## Release Manager / DevOps or Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers, DevOps engineers, and SREs reduce rollout risk by coordinating delivery readiness, production stability, and operational recovery. They keep release quality and reliability visible as part of the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment timing, change windows, and rollback plans
- Ensure observability, monitoring, and recovery readiness for production systems
- Validate staging and production health before and after release
- Partner with Project Manager on release planning and communication
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm release readiness
- Support operational incident response and post-release review

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and production disruption
- Improve system reliability and recovery readiness
- Support smooth communication and operational clarity during launches

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Release and deployment checklists
- Incident response and post-deploy verification
- Coordination with Stakeholders on launch updates and known issues

---

## Security, Privacy, or Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security, Privacy, and Compliance roles help teams identify risk early, maintain trust, and ensure work meets required standards. They act as a safeguard for technical, legal, and governance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review work for security, privacy, and regulatory concerns
- Identify controls, obligations, and risk mitigations early in the lifecycle
- Support engineering and product decisions with policy guidance
- Track findings, approvals, and remediation progress
- Advise Project Manager on escalation paths and risk impacts
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to validate controls and evidence

### Goals
- Reduce organizational and customer risk
- Improve confidence in launch readiness and operational safety
- Align delivery with approved standards and policies

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy reviews
- Risk assessments and remediation discussions
- Escalation to PM and stakeholders when issues affect timeline or scope
- Collaboration during planning, release, and incident response

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring frontline insight into the real-world experience of customers and operational teams. They ensure product changes are practical, supportable, and ready for rollout.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer pain points, recurring issues, and support trends
- Help validate whether the solution is supportable and operationally practical
- Participate in readiness reviews for launches and major changes
- Support stakeholder communication and customer-facing guidance
- Partner with Product Manager on customer impact and prioritization
- Coordinate with Release Manager on launch readiness and post-release support

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and service continuity
- Reduce friction between product delivery and operational support
- Ensure new releases are ready for real-world usage

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and support discussions
- Launch readiness and operational handoff meetings
- Collaboration with Product Manager and Stakeholders on communication needs

---

## Data / Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics partners help measure whether the project is achieving the intended outcome. They define metrics, instrumentation, and reporting needed to assess success over time.

### Responsibilities
- Define key success metrics and measurement approach
- Work with Developers to implement telemetry and event tracking
- Partner with Product Manager to align metrics with business outcomes
- Monitor dashboards and reporting for early signals and trends
- Support milestone reviews and retrospective analysis with evidence
- Help identify if the project is meeting expected value and where course correction is needed

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and visible
- Improve decision-making with evidence
- Strengthen learning and iteration across project milestones

### Typical Communication
- KPI definitions and dashboard reviews
- Alignment with Product Manager on success criteria
- Collaboration with Developers on instrumentation and data quality
- Status reporting to PM and Stakeholders

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Keep the set lightweight and practical: add only the roles that materially affect ownership, decisions, delivery, quality, and stakeholder alignment.
